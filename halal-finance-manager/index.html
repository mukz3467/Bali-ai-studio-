GUIDE & TRANSPARENCY

How to use this halal crypto market monitor

Halal Finance Manager Pro is a market-monitoring tool for people who want to review crypto markets while keeping their own Shariah screening process separate from price analysis. The watchlist starts empty: add an asset only after checking it against a credible methodology you trust. The app records your decision but does not issue a fatwa or independently certify any token.

Market data, estimates, and backtesting

The dashboard reads public Binance Spot market data. Volatility ranges and directional signals are statistical estimates, not guaranteed forecasts. The Quant Lab uses a transparent momentum heuristic and a walk-forward comparison; historical performance does not prove future profitability. Consider actual trading fees, spread, slippage, liquidity, and downside risk before making any manual decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does this app automatically decide which coins are halal?

No. It does not certify assets or provide a fatwa. You must independently review each asset under your chosen Shariah standard and leave uncertain assets off the watchlist.

Does the app place Binance trades automatically?

No. It uses public market data and provides informational alerts only. It does not place orders and does not need Binance trading or withdrawal API keys.

Are price estimates or backtest results guaranteed to be accurate?

No. Estimates use a transparent heuristic, and historical tests are limited samples. Results can change by market regime and do not guarantee a correct signal or profit.
